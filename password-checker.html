Tips for Strong Passwords

  • Use a passphrase of 4 or more random words, then add a number and a symbol.
  • Use a different password for every account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever possible.
  • Use a password manager so you don't have to remember everything.
  • Never use your name, birthday or phone number.

How it works

The checker looks at the length and the mix of character types, then estimates the strength in "bits". Every extra bit doubles the number of guesses an attacker needs. The time shown assumes an attacker making 10 billion guesses per second.
